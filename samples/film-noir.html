Don't Lie

The Setup

Rain-slicked streets reflect the glow of a single streetlight. A figure emerges from the fog, collar turned up against the cold.

Chapter II

The Confrontation

Smoke curls from a cigarette in the darkness. Light cuts through venetian blinds, striping the room in bars of shadow and revelation.

Chapter III

The Resolution

Every shadow tells a story. In the end, it's not the darkness that's dangerous but what you find when you shine a light into it.
