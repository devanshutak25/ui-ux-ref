RGB Channel Displacement

When light bends wrong, beauty happens. Red and cyan split apart, creating a visual tension that sits between error and art.

R

Red Channel

Shifted left and down, the red layer bleeds out from behind text and edges, creating a warm ghost image.

G

Green Channel

The anchor channel. Stays centered while red and blue pull apart, holding the core image together.

B

Blue / Cyan

Offset right and up, the cyan complement creates the other half of the split, completing the chromatic effect.
