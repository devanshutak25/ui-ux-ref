Paper Fold Design

Clean geometry meets soft materiality. Folded surfaces create depth through shadow, crease, and the subtle art of paper architecture.

01

Valley Fold

The most fundamental fold in origami, creating a crease where the paper bends toward you, forming valleys of shadow.

02

Mountain Fold

The complement to the valley fold, paper bends away from you, creating peaks that catch light and cast gentle shadows.

03

Reverse Fold

A fold within a fold, creating complexity from simplicity. Layers build upon layers to form intricate paper structures.
