Hierarchy

Guide the eye through size, weight, and color. The most important thing should be the biggest thing.

Chapter 1

Contrast

Light against dark. Big against small. Without contrast, nothing stands out and everything is noise.

Chapter 2

Alignment

Every element should have a visual connection to something else on the page. Nothing should look arbitrary.

Chapter 3

Repetition

Consistency builds trust. Repeat colors, fonts, and spacing to create a unified, professional feel.
