Bold lines cut into blocks. Ink pressed onto paper. The ancient art of relief printing — where every mark is deliberate and every surface tells a story.

The Process
From block to print
01

Carve

The image is drawn in reverse on a smooth block of wood or linoleum. Gouges remove the non-printing areas, leaving raised surfaces.

02

Ink

A brayer rolls oil-based ink across the surface. Only the raised areas receive pigment. The carved valleys remain untouched.

03

Press

Paper meets block under pressure. The ink transfers, creating a mirror of the carved image. Each print is unique — slightly imperfect, always alive.

"The woodcut is the most original graphic technique. It lives in the tension between what is carved away and what remains."
